# Iron Anvil Experimental 1.4.3 — automatic woodland screening

Automatic ground placement now avoids mapped woodland. **No new settings, map uploads, accounts or runtime downloads.**

- Bundled offline tree-cover masks cover Caucasus, Syria and Cold War Germany.
- The check covers a site's full footprint and a clearance margin, including wooded patches between the centre and its edges.
- Automatic supporting sites, reserve staging, convoy endpoints and nearby defensive positions use the screening too.
- For an explicitly selected isolated departure, bounded retries can choose a longer clear-ground sortie when nearby terrain cannot fit the mission. The briefing explains the longer distance.
- Manual map pins remain deliberate overrides. **Use Clear target when loading an old pinned preset** to let the generator choose a screened new location. A flagged pin is explained in the briefing.

The native enemy CAP launch, spawn confirmation and waypoint timing fixes remain included. No campaign systems, extra controls or terrain-export tools were added.

## [Download IronAnvil-Experimental.exe](https://github.com/SkylerErcoli/DCSMissionGeneratorJamesIronDemonAnvil/releases/download/v1.4.3-experimental.9/IronAnvil-Experimental.exe)

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Close the old app, replace its EXE and generate a **new** mission. Saved preferences remain available. Existing `.miz` files retain their old placements.

The masks add about 7 MB of bundled data. They are derived from [ESA WorldCover 2021](https://esa-worldcover.org/en/data-access), credited under CC BY 4.0, and sampled at approximately 62–111 m. **Real-world trees can differ from DCS, especially historical Germany.** This is a practical woodland filter, not a guarantee that every individual DCS tree is avoided. It screens initial ground placement; DCS still controls movement along roads. Fixed DCS airfield spawns are retained.

Checks cover known wooded/open locations, complete footprints, data boundaries, geographic variation and actual enemy ground objects across all maps/mission types. The compiled Windows probe validates bundled masks and saved mission placements; reports and checksum are attached. Actual DCS flight confirmation remains outstanding. The EXE is unsigned. Stable 1.2 and all previous releases are unchanged.
