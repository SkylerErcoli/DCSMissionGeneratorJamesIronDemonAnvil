# Iron Anvil Experimental 1.4.1 — fix DCS flight route timing

Fixes the DCS **“Mission cannot be saved due to errors!”** message naming **TEXACO, OVERLORD and ENEMY CAP 1**. The generator had unlocked every waypoint's arrival time on support and CAP routes. DCS requires a locked time reference, specifically at the first waypoint for late-activated flights.

## What's changed

- Tankers, AWACS and enemy CAP now retain a **locked first waypoint at time zero**. Later legs remain speed-controlled. Orbit anchors, cruising speeds, altitudes, support protection and CAP activation are preserved.
- Regression tests reproduce the reported Caucasus Supply Denial mission and its exported settings. Support-route tests inspect actual saved archives across all three maps and five support aircraft variants; CAP tests cover immediate and late activation.
- The portable EXE's release probe now checks **every plane and helicopter route in 25 generated missions** for the DCS timing requirements. A pydcs reload alone did not detect the original error.
