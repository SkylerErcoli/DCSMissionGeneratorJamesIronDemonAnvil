# Iron Anvil 1.2.0

A more comfortable mission planner, more varied targets, and fixes for enemy fighters and friendly support aircraft.

## What's new

- **Dark mode:** switch between dark and light themes. The app remembers your choice and your mission settings.
- **Every airfield and helipad in the supported map data:** 21 on Caucasus, 224 on Syria, and 227 on Cold War Germany. Search for your departure base; the app checks aircraft and parking compatibility. Your selected home base becomes friendly, with fuel and ammunition available.
- **More weather:** clear skies, scattered clouds, broken clouds, overcast, rain, thunderstorms, fog, strong winds, and random moderate conditions.
- **Easier updates:** install over your existing copy without uninstalling. Your settings and generated missions stay in place. The new **Check for updates** button downloads and verifies future updates before opening the installer.
- **More interesting facility strikes:** choose fuel depots, command centers, ammunition depots, factories, radar stations, or a random facility. Difficulty creates two to four separated occupied sites with guards, parked vehicles, equipment, tents, containers, and supplies.

## Fixes and mission improvements

- **Enemy CAP activation fixed:** enemy fighters activate after a player takes off or joins an airborne slot. Activation retries when needed, and fighters cannot count as defeated before they have spawned.
- **Tanker and AWACS routes rebuilt:** complete racetrack orbits, safe cruise settings, and aligned starting headings address the reported dives at mission start.
- **Friendly support protected:** tanker and AWACS aircraft are hidden from enemy AI targeting and protected from damage. They remain visible to players and retain their support roles.
- **Facility variety restored:** targets have distinct layouts and functions. A factory complex contains at most one giant workshop building.
- **Helicopter return routes fixed** for helicopter-only bases without runways. Fortified lines retain staggered troops and sandbag cover.
