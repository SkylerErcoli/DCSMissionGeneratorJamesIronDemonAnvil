# Iron Anvil Experimental 1.4.4 — enemy factions and helicopter sorties

Choose **Customize → Mission → Enemy faction** to fly against Russian regulars, the Syrian army, a Western task force or insurgent militia. All four work on Caucasus, Syria and Cold War Germany.

- Factions change actual vehicles, infantry, defenses and armed fighters. Western enemies stay RED; your departure and support stay friendly.
- Militia use lighter/captured equipment. If you enable enemy fighters, supporting Syrian MiG-21s provide CAP. CAP still launches after a client climbs above about 100 ft AGL.
- **Helicopter scale is automatic.** Automatic target distances are roughly 8 / 16 / 28 km for Close / Standard / Longer sorties, with variation. Linked sites and ground-response routes are closer; convoys and garrisons are smaller. A constrained selected departure can still require a longer route, explained in the briefing.
- Normal helicopter defenses use guns; Hard adds MANPADS and Extreme can include SAMs. Your fighter/air-defense switches still work independently. Selected tank targets, factory targets and manual pins remain deliberate choices.
- Helicopter random facilities favor fuel, command, ammunition and radar objectives. Supporting compounds, occupants and scenery remain included. Cooperative convoy goals still require two of the three supply trucks.
- Recommended weapons for militia air-defense or unarmed support targets use ground-attack stores, so the flight can attack targets without radar emitters.
- The mission briefing identifies your faction and scale. Saved settings/mission imports retain the faction. Older settings keep their existing regular/insurgent selection.

## [Download IronAnvil-Experimental.exe](https://github.com/SkylerErcoli/DCSMissionGeneratorJamesIronDemonAnvil/releases/download/v1.4.4-experimental.10/IronAnvil-Experimental.exe)

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Close the old app, replace its EXE and generate a **new** mission. No installer, Python, extra aircraft modules or new map files are required. Preferences and existing missions remain available; old `.miz` files retain their original forces.

Offline woodland screening, protected support orbits and the CAP/waypoint timing fixes remain included. Native Windows validation reports and SHA-256 are attached. Actual DCS flight testing remains outstanding; public terrain/trees and stock AI behavior need in-simulator checks. Factions are practical equipment presets, not exact historical orders of battle. The EXE is unsigned. Stable 1.2 and all previous releases are unchanged.
