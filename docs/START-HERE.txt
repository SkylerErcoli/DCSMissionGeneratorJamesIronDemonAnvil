IRON ANVIL MISSIONS
==================

If you downloaded IronAnvil-Setup-x64.exe:
1. Double-click it and follow the short setup wizard.
2. Open Iron Anvil from the Start menu or its desktop shortcut.

If you downloaded IronAnvil-Windows-x64.zip instead:
1. Extract the whole ZIP with Windows "Extract All".
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click IronAnvil.exe.
3. Keep IronAnvil.exe and the _internal folder together.

Use Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. No Python installation or typed commands are
needed. The installer works without administrator access.

MAKE A MISSION
--------------
Choose a map, aircraft, mission, and number of pilots (1 to 4).

Enemy CAP flights = enemy fighter patrols.
Enemy air defenses = enemy missiles and anti-aircraft guns.
Either checkbox can be turned on or off for every mission type.

Click the map to choose the target location, or leave it unset for the app
to choose. Use the mouse wheel to zoom. Drag to move around the map.
"Internet map (OSM)" adds roads and towns when you have internet access.
It shows real-world geography, which can differ from DCS scenery.

Click "Generate mission", then "Open mission folder".
Open the new .miz file in DCS or use it to host a multiplayer mission.

Only the mission creator needs this app. Friends join through DCS as usual.
Everyone flying needs their chosen aircraft and the selected DCS map.
Syria and Cold War Germany require their separate DCS map modules.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST GROUP FLIGHT
-----------------------------
Check the generated mission's target placement and aircraft loadouts in
the DCS Mission Editor. Full flight testing in DCS remains outstanding.

IF THE APP DOES NOT OPEN
------------------------
Run the installer again. For the portable ZIP, extract the entire ZIP
before running the app. Keep IronAnvil.exe beside its _internal folder.
