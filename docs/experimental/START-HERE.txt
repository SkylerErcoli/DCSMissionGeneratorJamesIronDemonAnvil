IRON ANVIL EXPERIMENTAL 1.4.0 — PORTABLE WINDOWS EXE

Download IronAnvil-Experimental.exe, save it locally, and double-click it.
No installer, ZIP extraction, Python or terminal is needed.

1. Quick setup: choose your map, shared aircraft, pilot count and mission.
2. Preview mission on map, then Generate mission. Open Mission briefing.
3. Host the .miz in DCS. Friends only need the chosen DCS map/aircraft modules.
4. F10 > Iron Anvil > Briefing / help repeats the job and recovery instructions.
5. Complete the required job and recover all survivors at home or the alternate.

Defaults: engines OFF in parking, nearby target, daylight, clear weather,
recommended weapons and Balanced opposition. Existing preferences are retained;
Use recommended setup applies the defaults. Relaxed removes optional threats.

Automatic missions vary departure, target region, direction and distance.
Offline terrain/roads help planning. Preview matches Generate, and the next
automatic Generate gets a fresh variation. New variation previews another plan.
A map pin or explicit replay seed stays fixed until you change it.

Broken Relay: disable fuel; optional command support delays the ground response.
Supply Denial: disable ammunition; bonus fuel can cancel a preparing response.
Rolling Thunder: stop two supply trucks before delivery for a bonus.
Facility strike chooses varied facility types. Guards/scenery are not mandatory
cleanup targets for the cooperative operations.

F10 Target assistance has live coordinates/bearings and optional orange smoke.
Smoke needs confirmed intelligence and an airborne teammate within 15 km;
the shared cooldown is five minutes. Convoys can move away from the smoke.

Update: close the app and replace the EXE. Saved preferences/missions stay put.
Previously used an installer? Open this new EXE directly. An old Start shortcut
still points to the old installed app. Stable 1.2 remains separate and untouched.

No DCS flight has been performed here. Terrain, roads, AI and multiplayer need
real flights. Public map/height data are approximate, not actual DCS terrain.
