IRON ANVIL EXPERIMENTAL 1.3.3 - EASIER MISSION SETUP

Stable Iron Anvil 1.2 stays installed separately.
Run this installer over an older experimental copy to update it in place.
No Python, terminal, extra client mods or DCS scripting changes are needed.

1. Open Iron Anvil Experimental from Start.
   For the ZIP, choose Extract All and keep the complete folder together.
2. Quick setup: choose your map, aircraft, pilot count and mission.
3. Choose Relaxed, Balanced or Challenging. Generate mission, or Preview first.
   The default is a nearby daytime mission with clear weather and engines running.
   Relaxed removes optional fighters, air defenses and the ground response.
   Ground troops remain armed. The two visible threat switches can be changed.
4. Open Mission briefing, then host the .miz in DCS. Friends need the selected
   DCS map and aircraft modules; only the mission creator needs this app.
5. In DCS: F10 > Iron Anvil > Briefing / help explains your job and recovery.
   Briefed targets are marked automatically. Complete the required goal and
   land all surviving participating aircraft at home or the approved alternate.

Broken Relay: disable two fuel tanks; an optional command site delays response.
Supply Denial: disable ammunition; bonus fuel support can cancel response.
Rolling Thunder: stop two cargo trucks, preferably before their supplies arrive.
Guards, escorts and scenery never block these operations' required objectives.

Customize contains departure airfields, starts, weather, weapons, recovery,
optional search intelligence, saved presets and settings import/export.
Repeat last mission is under Customize > Flight > Squadron presets.
New variation previews another layout. Generate uses that preview's variation.
A map pin keeps the target fixed and overrides the automatic distance.

No flight in DCS has been performed here. Check a new mission in Mission Editor
before a group sortie; terrain, roads, damage and multiplayer behavior need flights.
