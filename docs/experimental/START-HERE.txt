IRON ANVIL EXPERIMENTAL 1.3.2 - CONVOYS, RECOVERY AND INTELLIGENCE

Stable Iron Anvil 1.2 stays installed separately.
No Python, terminal, extra DCS scripts or extra aircraft/map modules are needed.

1. Run the experimental installer, then open Iron Anvil Experimental from Start.
   Already have the first preview? Run this installer over it to update in place.
   For the ZIP instead, choose Extract All and keep the entire folder together.
2. Choose the DCS map and aircraft your group already owns, and 1-4 pilots.
3. Mission tab: choose Broken Relay, Supply Denial, Rolling Thunder, or Classic.
   Broken Relay: destroy 2 of 3 fuel tanks. A bonus command structure delays the
   ground response by six minutes if destroyed before dispatch.
   Supply Denial: disable an ammunition structure. A bonus fuel tank cancels
   the ground response if destroyed before dispatch.
   Rolling Thunder: stop 2 of 3 moving supply trucks. Stop them before delivery
   for extra points; delivery accelerates a still-preparing response. Bonus fuel
   support can cancel the response before dispatch. Escorts are not required kills.
4. Leave targets automatic for the selected distance or pin one on the map.
   CAP, air defenses and the finite ground response have independent switches.
   Optional search intelligence gives an approximate area: keep a teammate
   airborne inside it for 45 continuous seconds, then use F10 Recon check-in.
   This is a radio-intelligence rule, not automatic sensor recognition.
5. Preview mission on map shows the briefed plan and selects a variation number.
   Generate uses that same plan. Changing settings clears it. Mission briefing
   opens objectives, radios and a schematic. A matching DCS kneeboard is included.
6. Host the .miz in DCS. Only the host needs the generator; friends need the
   selected DCS modules. All pilots can fly the same aircraft type.
7. Complete the main objective and recover every surviving participating pilot.
   Flight tab can approve a friendly alternate as well as home. F10 gives vectors.
   If things go badly, agree over comms and use F10 > Iron Anvil > Team withdrawal:
   Propose, then Confirm within 30 seconds; any teammate can cancel the proposal.
   Enemies remain active. Recover everyone safely for the partial debrief.

Save named squadron presets or export settings to share a favorite setup.
Repeat last mission loads its settings and seed. Change the variation number for
another layout. Importing a friend's settings keeps your own output folder.

Experimental settings and missions are separate from stable Iron Anvil.
Experimental downloads opens the dedicated prerelease, not the stable updater.

Automated checks do not replace a flight in DCS. Check new missions in the Mission
Editor before your first group sortie. Terrain/road AI, object damage behavior and
in-simulator kneeboard display still need real DCS playtesting.
