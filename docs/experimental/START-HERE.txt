IRON ANVIL EXPERIMENTAL 1.3 - FIRST CO-OP PREVIEW

This is the experimental app. Stable Iron Anvil 1.2 stays installed separately.
No Python, terminal, extra DCS scripts or extra aircraft/map modules are needed.

1. If you downloaded the ZIP, choose Extract All and keep the whole folder together.
   Double-click IronAnvil.exe. The installer instead creates an Iron Anvil
   Experimental Start menu entry and an optional desktop shortcut.
2. Choose the DCS map and aircraft your group already owns, and 1-4 pilots.
3. Mission tab: choose Broken Relay, Supply Denial, or Classic single mission.
   Broken Relay: destroy 2 of 3 fuel tanks; disabling one command structure first
   delays the staged ground response by six minutes.
   Supply Denial: disable one ammunition structure; disabling one bonus fuel tank
   before dispatch cancels the ground response.
4. Leave the target automatic for the selected distance, or pin it on the map.
   CAP and air-defense switches still work independently. Ground response is optional.
5. Generate the mission. Mission briefing opens objectives, radios, distance and
   a route schematic. The same information is in the mission's DCS kneeboard.
6. Host the .miz in DCS. All players share the objectives and use the same selected
   aircraft type. Complete the required goal and bring all surviving teammates home.
   F10 > Iron Anvil includes operation status and response staging marks.

Squadron presets and exported settings make favorite setups easy to reuse.
Repeat last mission loads its settings and seed. Use a new variation number for
another layout. Importing a friend's settings keeps your own output folder.

Experimental settings and missions are separate from stable Iron Anvil.
Experimental downloads opens the dedicated prerelease, not the stable updater.
Run a newer experimental installer over this copy to update it in place.

Automated checks do not replace a flight in DCS. Check new missions in the Mission
Editor before your first group sortie. Terrain/road AI, object damage behavior and
in-simulator kneeboard display still need real DCS playtesting.
